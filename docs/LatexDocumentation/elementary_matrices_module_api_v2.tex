\section{Elementary Matrices}
\label{sec:elementary_matrices}

\begin{center}
\code{bionetflux.utils.elementary\_matrices} \quad($\sim$230 lines)
\end{center}

All element-level HDG matrices are computed once on the reference interval
$[0,1]$ using SymPy symbolic integration, then stored as NumPy arrays.

% ------------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection{Constructor}

\begin{lstlisting}[language=Python, caption={ElementaryMatrices constructor}]
class ElementaryMatrices:
    def __init__(self, orthonormal_basis: bool = False):
\end{lstlisting}

\noindent Only the Lagrange basis (\code{orthonormal\_basis=False}) is operational.
Passing \code{orthonormal\_basis=True} raises \code{NotImplementedError}.

With the standard Lagrange basis on $[0,1]$:
\[
  e_0(y) = 1-y, \qquad e_1(y) = y, \qquad n_0 = -1, \quad n_1 = +1.
\]

% ------------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection{Matrix Catalogue}

All matrices are accessed via \code{get\_matrix(name)}.

\begin{longtable}{p{2cm}p{4.5cm}p{7cm}}
\toprule
\textbf{Key} & \textbf{Definition} & \textbf{Description} \\
\midrule
\code{M} & $M_{ij} = \int_0^1 e_j\, e_i\, dy$ & Mass matrix ($2\times 2$) \\
\code{IM} & $M^{-1}$ & Inverse of the mass matrix \\
\code{D} & $D_{ij} = \int_0^1 e_j\, e_i'\, dy$ & Derivative matrix \\
\code{T} & $T_{ij} = e_j(x_i)$ & Trace matrix (evaluation at endpoints) \\
\code{Gb} & $\text{Gb}_{ij} = e_i(0)\hat e_j(0) + e_i(1)\hat e_j(1)$ & Boundary Gramian \\
\code{Mb} & $\text{Mb}_{ij} = e_i(0)e_j(0) + e_i(1)e_j(1)$ & Boundary mass matrix \\
\code{Ntil} & $\tilde N_{ij} = e_j(0)e_i(0)n_0 + e_j(1)e_i(1)n_1$ & Normal matrix \\
\code{Nhat} & $\hat N = \begin{pmatrix}-1&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix}$ & Hat-normal matrix \\
\code{Z} & $0_{2\times 2}$ & Zero matrix \\
\code{Av} & $\mathbf{1}^T T^{-1} M$ & Averaging operator (row) \\
\code{QUAD} & $Q_{ij} = e_i(\xi_j)\,w_j / 2$ & Quadrature matrix (4-point LGL) \\
\code{qnodes} & $[-1, -0.447, 0.447, 1]$ & LGL nodes on $[-1,1]$ \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

% ------------------------------------------------------------------
\subsection{Built-in Verification}

The constructor checks the following identities:
\begin{align}
  D + D^T - \tilde N &= 0 & &\text{(integration by parts)}, \\
  (\tilde N - D)\, M^{-1}\,(D - \hat N\, T) &= 0 & &\text{($\partial_{xx}=0$ for linear)}, \\
  \text{Mb} - \text{Gb}\, T &= 0 & &\text{(boundary consistency)}.
\end{align}

Results are stored in \code{test\_integration\_by\_parts},
\code{test\_dxx\_zero}, and \code{test\_mb\_gb} and can be printed with
\code{print\_tests()}.
